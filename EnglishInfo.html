About Me

Driving Project Excellence

As a dedicated Planning and Project Control Manager with a Master of Science in Industrial Engineering, I specialize in transforming complex project requirements into structured, achievable milestones.

My expertise lies in leveraging advanced scheduling tools and analytical methodologies to ensure projects are delivered on time, within scope, and under budget. I am committed to driving operational excellence through meticulous Earned Value Management, risk mitigation, and resource optimization.
